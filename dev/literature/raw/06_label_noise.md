# Review item 6: label noise and label disagreement in sepsis prediction; noisy and weak-label methods (search report, 31 Oct 2026)

Access caveat recorded by the searcher: snippets and abstracts only; Nature, arXiv and PMC full texts not reachable; search budget ran out before verifying Seymour 2016 window details and a MIMIC-IV ICD-versus-Sepsis-3 overlap study; [UNVERIFIED] items from memory or secondary summaries.

## Operational Sepsis-3 versus coding versus adjudication

- Johnson 2018 (MIMIC-III, 11,791 stays): Sepsis-3 at ICU admission 49.1 %; CDC adult sepsis event 31.9 %; Angus 28.6 %; Martin 14.7 %; CMS 11.0 %; explicit ICD codes 9.0 %. Operational Sepsis-3 captures about five times more stays than explicit coding; our 35 to 50 % documentation rate is high by that standard.
- Rhee 2017 (JAMA, 409 hospitals): against chart review, EHR clinical criteria sensitivity 69.7 % versus claims 32.3 %; PPV 70.4 % versus 75.2 %. Coding is specific and misses about two thirds of cases. Jolley 2015: sepsis highly under-coded.
- MARS cohort (Utrecht) [author UNVERIFIED]: of 1,081 admissions with suspected infection, 90 % met Sepsis-3 versus 60 % by prospective physician adjudication: a third of operational positives are adjudication-negative. Lindner 2022: Sepsis-3 sensitivity about 70 %, specificity about 92 % against daily attending labels. Henry 2019: Sepsis-3 produced about twice the false positives of an EHR phenotype that was 79 % physician-confirmed. Valik 2020 (wards): automated Sepsis-3 sensitivity 0.887, PPV 0.881, so the operational label is cleaner outside the ICU.
- Clinician labels are noisy too: Rhee 2016 vignette study, Fleiss kappa 0.29 among 94 intensivists; Lopansri 2019 kappa 0.58 to 0.68; Rhee 2018: reviewers agreed on sepsis time zero in only 36 % of 80 cases, worse for hospital-onset sepsis.
- Circularity: Moor 2021 (22 studies); Hagmann, Schamoni and Riezler 2023: 21 of 22 studies derive labels from the same measurements used as features; Schamoni 2019 trained on physician-questionnaire labels as a non-circular alternative.

## Sensitivity to the definition, horizon and suspicion window

- Cohen 2024 (Sci Rep, MIMIC-III): three readings of Sepsis-3 onset give 867 to 2,178 septic stays; AUROC varies 0 to 6 points across definitions versus 1 to 5 across models; label choice moves results more than architecture.
- Moor 2021 and 2023: the alternative suspected-infection rule (antibiotics only, used where sampling is missing, as in eICU) had Jaccard 0.42 with the standard rule on AUMC; ricu defaults si_lwr 48 h, si_upr 24 h, SOFA threshold 2 [sep3_alt mapping UNVERIFIED].
- Fleuren 2020: 28 papers, ICU AUROC 0.68 to 0.99, definitions too heterogeneous to pool. Lauritsen 2021: four framings of one dataset gave AUROC 0.73 to 0.82 but average precision 0.007 to 0.385.
- Treatment leakage: Kamran 2024 (NEJM AI): Epic model AUROC 0.62 overall, 0.47 when predictions after clinical recognition are excluded; treatment preceded criteria in about 85 % of cases [UNVERIFIED]. Wong 2021: AUROC 0.63 versus vendor 0.76 to 0.83. PhysioNet 2019 shifted Sepsis-3 six hours earlier for this reason. Do 2026: MIMIC-IV 6-hour AUROC 0.84 fell to 0.67 externally.

## Noisy and weak-label methods

- Generic: co-teaching (Han 2018), bootstrapping loss (Reed 2015), label smoothing (Lukasik 2020: benefit mainly for symmetric noise), confident learning (Northcutt 2021), positive-unlabelled learning (Elkan and Noto 2008; Bekker and Davis 2020), Snorkel label models (Ratner 2017).
- Clinical: Wei 2024 (JAMIA scoping review, 60 studies, mostly imaging). Tjandra and Wiens 2024 (survival analysis with multiple noisy labels, applied to sepsis onset where CDC and Sepsis-3 disagree). Li, Luo and Aickelin 2025 (ACTLL, EHR time series on MIMIC-IV-ED and eICU) [gain UNVERIFIED]. de Vos 2023 (stochastic co-teaching on ECG); a 2025 thesis found no gain for co-teaching on PTB-XL. Yang 2024: robust losses discard informative hard cases. Saab 2020: weak supervision on EEG. Anchor and PU phenotyping: Zhang 2020 (JAMIA), Halpern 2016 [UNVERIFIED]. Vogel and Cordier 2025: PU learning recovers uncoded sepsis at rates matching chart review. No published sepsis time-series study uses co-teaching, label smoothing or bootstrapping.

## Which approach fits our setting

The noise is instance-dependent and one-sided: documentation is a high-PPV, low-sensitivity second label (an anchor) and the operational positive class is a mixture. This is the anchor or positive-unlabelled setting, not the symmetric-flip setting where co-teaching, smoothing and bootstrapping were validated. Best fits: (i) label-stratified training with a cross-evaluation matrix (train on operational, on documented-and-operational, on a soft label; evaluate on each), the Cohen 2024 design applied to our two labels; (ii) PU-style soft labels (documented positives 1, undocumented operational positives the class prior 0.35 to 0.50, controls 0; weighted BCE or nnPU), noting that documentation is not selected at random; (iii) confident learning with out-of-fold probabilities to down-weight implausible positives; (iv) a Snorkel label model is feasible with the available labelling functions but there is no adjudicated validation set. For the antibiotic lead: Kamran-style evaluation excluding post-treatment predictions.

## Expected gain, cost, recommendation

- No sepsis-specific benchmark; generic gains 2 to 5 AUC points under 20 to 40 % synthetic noise, often nil under real clinical noise. The honest gain is a clearer target rather than a higher AUROC on the operational label. Searcher's derivation: if a fraction rho of labelled positives is indistinguishable from controls, AUROC against that label is bounded near 1 − rho/2; with rho 0.5 to 0.65 the ceiling is about 0.68 to 0.75, consistent with reported 6-hour ICU AUROCs of 0.75 to 0.85. Expect 3 to 8 points on documented-positive evaluation; co-teaching or smoothing at most 1 point.
- Cost: stratified arms plus cross-evaluation 1 to 2 weeks mostly label engineering, 2 to 4 extra runs per model; PU soft labels 1 week, same compute; confident learning 2 to 4 days at twice the compute; Snorkel 1 to 2 weeks; co-teaching 1 week at twice the networks with low return; multiple-instance or noisy-label survival 3 to 6 weeks.
- Recommendation: add as a prespecified secondary arm plus sensitivity analysis: (a) label-stratified training with cross-evaluation on operational, documented and ICD labels; (b) one cheap noise-aware variant (PU soft labels or confident-learning pruning); (c) Kamran-style evaluation excluding predictions after the first antibiotic. Keep co-teaching, label smoothing and bootstrapping for discussion; drop multiple-instance and noisy-label survival unless onset timing becomes its own study. Frame with Cohen 2024 and the adjudication literature.
